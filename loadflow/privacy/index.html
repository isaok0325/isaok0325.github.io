LoadFlow is made by an independent developer. There is no LoadFlow account and no LoadFlow server: your training records stay on your iPhone and, if you turn on sync, in your own private iCloud storage. The developer cannot read them.

Who is responsible

Isao Kataoka / 片岡　功 ("we"), an independent developer in Japan — the same name shown as the seller of LoadFlow on the App Store. Questions about this policy or your data: loadflow.support@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Everything you record — exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, notes, workout plans, body measurements, and your settings — is stored locally on your iPhone.

If iCloud sync is on, the same records are also stored in the private database of your own iCloud account, so they can move between your devices. That storage belongs to your Apple Account; we have no access to it and receive no copy. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Exporting to CSV writes a file that you then share wherever you choose. Once you share it, the destination app or service handles it under its own terms.

Apple Health

Apple Health integration is off until you turn it on and grant permission. When enabled, LoadFlow writes body weight, body fat percentage and completed workouts to Health. This version does not read anything from Health. Health data is never sent to us or to any third party, and is never used for analytics or advertising.

Usage analytics

To understand which features are used and where people get stuck, LoadFlow can send anonymous usage events through Google Firebase Analytics. Nothing is sent until the app knows whether it may collect. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Data → Share Usage Data; while it is off, nothing is sent, and anything the app was holding is discarded. In the EEA and the UK, collection additionally follows the choice you make in the consent screen shown on first launch — declining there means nothing is sent, and you can reopen that choice in Settings → Ad Privacy Settings.

What is sent:

  • Which screens are opened, and that a workout was started or finished
  • How many exercises and sets a session contained, and which input mode was used
  • Whether a paywall was shown and from which part of the app, and whether a plan limit was reached
  • That a CSV export ran
  • Your training level, subscription state, unit system, app language, and whether Health sync is on

What is never sent:

  • Your name, email address or any account identifier — the app has no account
  • The weights, repetitions or body measurements you record, or the notes and exercise names you type
  • Anything from Apple Health
  • Your precise location

Events are tied to a Firebase installation identifier for the app on your device, not to you. Analytics data is retained for 14 months and then deleted. Google acts as our processor for this data; see Firebase's privacy information and Google's privacy policy.

Advertising

The free version shows occasional full-screen ads from Google AdMob between exercises and at the end of a session. Subscribers do not see ads.

In the EEA and the UK, the consent screen on first launch asks whether ads may be personalised and lists the ad partners involved; if you decline, ads are non-personalised. On iOS, personalised ads also require your permission in the separate "Allow tracking" prompt — declining it means ads are not based on tracking across other apps and websites. Where that consent screen applies, you can reopen it at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy Settings. Ad delivery involves device and ad identifiers and coarse information such as country and device type. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; we only receive from Apple the fact that a subscription is active, so the app can unlock paid features.

Children

LoadFlow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn usage analytics off at any time in Settings → Data → Share Usage Data. This works everywhere, whether or not consent is required where you live.
  • In the EEA and the UK, change your consent for ads and usage analytics at any time in Settings → Ad Privacy Settings.
  • On iPhone, withdraw permission for ad tracking at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Turn off Apple Health writing at any time in LoadFlow's settings, or in the Health app.
  • Export your records to CSV at any time.
  • Delete everything by deleting the app; to also remove the synced copy, delete LoadFlow's iCloud data from iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data. Because we hold no account and no server-side copy of your records, most of these you can exercise directly in the app; for anything else, write to loadflow.support@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the new version is published here with a new effective date, and the app points to this same address.
