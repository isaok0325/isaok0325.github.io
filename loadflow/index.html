Frequently asked

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase.

How do I cancel the free trial or the subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by LoadFlow: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the trial ends avoids the first charge.

Where is my data stored, and how do I delete it?

Your records stay on your iPhone and, if iCloud sync is on, in your own private iCloud database. Deleting the app removes the data on the device; to also remove the synced copy, turn off iCloud sync before deleting, or delete LoadFlow's data from iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud. See the privacy policy for details.

My workout data is not syncing between devices.

Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, that iCloud Drive is on, and that LoadFlow is allowed to use iCloud. Sync runs in the background and may take a few minutes.
